=======================================================
HOW TO ADD YOUR IMAGES, VIDEO & MUSIC FOR PATHU
=======================================================

You can place your files directly in this "assets" folder or in the main folder!
The website will automatically detect and load them.

FILE NAMES SUPPORTED:
-------------------------------------------------------
1. STAGE 1 & 2 BACKGROUND:
   - "star bg.png" or "star bg.jpg" or "star_bg.png"

2. STAGE 1 MIFFY IMAGE:
   - "MIFFY STAGE 1.png" or "MIFFY STAGE 1.jpg" or "miffy_stage_1.png"

3. STAGE 2 MIFFY GIF:
   - "miffy stage 2.gif" or "miffy_stage_2.gif"

4. STAGE 3 & 4 VIDEO BACKGROUND:
   - "cherry vid bg.mp4" or "cherry vid bg.webm" or "cherry_vid_bg.mp4"

5. STAGE 3 BOX & CAKE:
   - "STAGE 3 BOX.gif" or "STAGE 3 BOX.png"
   - "CAKE STAGE 3.png" or "CAKE STAGE 3.jpg"

6. STAGE 4 BOX & JELLYCAT:
   - "STAGE 4 BOX.gif" or "STAGE 4 BOX.png"
   - "JELLYCAT.png" or "JELLYCAT.jpg"

7. STAGE 5 BACKGROUND & BOX:
   - "dots bg.png" or "dots bg.jpg"
   - "STAGE 5 BOX.gif" or "STAGE 5 BOX.png"

8. MUSIC:
   - "music.mp3" or "birthday.mp3"

* If any file is missing, the website has built-in cute animated illustrations and a sweet music-box birthday chime, so everything looks gorgeous immediately!
* You can also click the "Customize / Upload" button in the bottom-right corner of the website to upload images and music directly from your computer with live preview!
